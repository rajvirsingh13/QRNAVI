Can I customize my QR code?

Yes. After generating a QR code, supported appearance settings can be adjusted using the QRNAVI customization editor.

Can I scan QR codes with QRNAVI?

Yes. QRNAVI includes a dedicated QR code scanner designed for compatible mobile and desktop browsers.
